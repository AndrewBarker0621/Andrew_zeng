Ruihao Zeng

Hi, I am Ruihao (Andrew) Zeng, a Ph.D. student in TransportLab, School of Civil Engineering, The University of Sydney, supervised by Assoc. Prof. Mohsen Ramezani and Prof. David Levinson. My Ph.D. (2024 – 2027) is supported by the Australian Government Research Training Program (RTP) Scholarship, and my thesis is on Distributed Intelligence in Systems of Autonomous Vehicles.

My research focuses on Distributed / Decentralised Autonomous Vehicles, Deep Reinforcement Learning for Multi-agent Vehicle Control, 3D Perception and Multi-object Tracking with LiDAR, and Spatio-temporal Traffic Prediction. I like building end-to-end systems, from data pipelines and large-scale simulation to real-time deployment in the cloud. I hope to use intelligent methods to solve problems in transportation and society.

Before my Ph.D., I completed an M.Phil. in Transportation at TransportLab (2022 – 2023) with High Distinction. My M.Phil. thesis was Online Multi-Object Tracking Using LiDAR, and I received the Paulette Isabel Jones Career Development Award. I received my B.E. degree in Digital Media Technology & Computer Science in 2022, graduating first in my cohort as the valedictorian.

From November 2020 to December 2022, I worked as a research assistant at the Institute of Transportation Big Data of Digital Fujian, co-advised by Xing Wang and Fumin Zou, developing deep-learning models for traffic flow prediction, trajectory prediction and traffic-anomaly detection on city-wide sensor data (7M+ records). I also worked for a summer research program on GAN-based line art colorization at Massachusetts Institute of Technology in 2021, supervised by Mark Vogelsberger.

Deep Reinforcement Learning for Communication-Free Distributed Control of Autonomous Vehicles in Unstructured Intersection
Ruihao Zeng and Mohsen Ramezani
IEEE Transactions on Cybernetics, 2026, Q1
[paper]

A continuous-PPO framework with curriculum learning and a reference-action-guided loss that controls acceleration and steering of autonomous vehicles in signal-free, lane-free intersections without any inter-vehicle communication. A rule-embedded hybrid policy supports long-term deployment safety: over 100M+ km of cumulative simulation, only 36 collisions were recorded.

TransportLab, The University of Sydney.

A Dynamic-Confidence 3D Multi-Object Tracking Method Based on Spatio-Temporal Association
Ruihao Zeng and Mohsen Ramezani
Measurement, vol. 256, 117864, 2025, Q1
[paper]

An online LiDAR-based 3D multi-object tracking method for moving sensors: a constant-acceleration motion model with orientation variation, a spatio-temporal feature-based data association model, and a dynamic confidence threshold to keep tracking temporarily occluded objects. Evaluated on KITTI and nuScenes.

Resulted from my M.Phil. thesis at TransportLab, The University of Sydney.

Projects
Multi-Agent Self-Adaptive Framework for Portfolio Risk Management
Feb. 2024 – Dec. 2024

A triple-agent reinforcement-learning architecture (a TD3-based decision agent, a convex-optimisation solver agent and a market-observer agent) coupled in a loosely-coupled pipelining model, with Jensen–Shannon-divergence-based reward shaping to prevent policy collapse under volatile markets. Achieved a 22.49% annual return and a Sharpe ratio of 0.92 on the S&P 500, and outperformed 10 baselines (incl. EIIE, RAT, PPN) across CSI 300, DJIA and S&P 500 over a 10-year horizon.

POLD: Parking-Lot Occupancy LiDAR Detection
Jan. 2023 – Jul. 2023, TransportLab & Seoul Robotics
[code]

A real-time LiDAR system that detects parking occupancy and visualises results through AWS-hosted web applications. Built EC2-based scripts for point-cloud parsing, map construction and automated parking-space recognition, and integrated the SENSR perception software with AWS into a complete data-acquisition-to-cloud-deployment pipeline.
